# SOP-EMR-301: Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) Thermal Event Response
Revision 1 | Owner: HSE and BESS Operations | Applies to: containerised BESS sites

## Purpose and Scope
This procedure covers the response to overheating or fire risk in battery containers. Thermal runaway can release toxic and flammable gases and can spread between racks.

## Warning Stages
Stage 1 alarm: any cell temperature above 60 degrees C or detection of off-gas. Operators must start monitoring and reduce power. Stage 2 alarm: any cell temperature above 70 degrees C. The battery management system isolates the rack and the HVAC runs at maximum.

## Immediate Actions
1. Raise the emergency alarm and inform the control room.
2. Evacuate all personnel to the muster point at least 50 m upwind of the container.
3. Do not open the container doors. Site staff must not enter the container.
4. Call the fire service. Only trained fire responders may apply water or suppression agents.
5. Keep the SCADA and BMS monitoring running from the control room.

## Re-entry Criteria
Do not re-enter until the gas monitor reads below the safe limit and the battery management system shows no cell above 45 degrees C for 2 hours. The HSE Manager must approve re-entry in writing.

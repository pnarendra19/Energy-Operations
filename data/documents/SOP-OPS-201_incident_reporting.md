# SOP-OPS-201: Incident Reporting and Escalation
Revision 5 | Owner: HSE and Operations | Applies to: all sites and contractors

## Purpose and Scope
This procedure sets how incidents, injuries, fires, equipment damage and near misses are reported and escalated. Prompt reporting protects people and preserves evidence.

## Severity Levels
S1: fatality, serious injury or fire. S2: lost-time injury or equipment damage above INR 10 lakh. S3: first-aid case or near miss with no injury.

## Reporting Timelines
For S1, notify the Site Manager immediately and the Regional Head within 1 hour. Submit a written preliminary report within 24 hours. For S2, notify the Site Manager within 4 hours and submit the report within 48 hours. For S3, log the event in the incident system by the end of the shift.

## Investigation
A root cause analysis using the 5-Why method must be completed within 7 days for S1 and S2 incidents. Preserve evidence: do not disturb the scene unless needed for safety, and save SCADA logs and alarm history for the affected period.

## Regulatory Notifications
Notifications to external authorities and regulators are made only by the Compliance team. Site staff must not contact regulators directly.

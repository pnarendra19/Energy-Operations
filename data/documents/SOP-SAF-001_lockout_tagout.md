# SOP-SAF-001: Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) for Electrical Isolation
Revision 3 | Owner: HSE Department | Applies to: wind, solar and substation assets

## Purpose and Scope
This procedure prevents accidental energisation during maintenance. It applies to every task that involves opening an electrical cabinet, inverter, converter, transformer or switchgear panel. No work may start without an approved Permit to Work (PTW).

## Roles
The Permit Issuer approves the PTW and confirms the isolation points. Each Authorised Person applies a personal lock and tag. The Shift In-charge keeps the LOTO register for the site.

## Isolation Procedure
1. Obtain the approved PTW from the Permit Issuer before starting any isolation.
2. Switch off the equipment and open every energy source, including grid breakers, DC isolators and auxiliary supplies.
3. Each Authorised Person attaches a personal lock and a tag showing name, date and PTW number to every isolation point.
4. Wait at least 10 minutes after isolation before opening converter or inverter cabinets, so the DC-link capacitors can discharge.

## Verification of Zero Energy
Use a voltage detector rated for the system voltage. Prove the detector on a known live source before the test and again after the test. Test every phase and earth. Apply temporary earthing where the PTW requires it. Never assume a circuit is dead because the switch is open.

## Removal of Locks
Only the person who applied a lock may remove it. If that person is unavailable, the Shift In-charge may authorise removal only after 3 documented attempts to contact them and with written approval from the Site Manager. Confirm that tools are cleared and all personnel are clear before re-energising.

## Shift Handover
Locks stay in place across shift changes. The incoming Authorised Person applies their own lock before the outgoing person removes theirs. The LOTO register must be updated at every handover.

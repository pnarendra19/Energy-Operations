# SOP-MNT-103: Power Transformer and Switchyard Inspection
Revision 3 | Owner: Substation Maintenance | Applies to: generator step-up transformers and switchyards

## Purpose and Scope
This procedure defines routine inspections and trip responses for power transformers and the switchyard. It supports reliable export of power to the grid.

## Daily Rounds
Operators perform a visual round every day. Check oil levels, leaks, bushings, cooling fans, breather condition and abnormal noise. Record readings in the substation log.

## Temperature Limits
Top-oil temperature: alarm at 85 degrees C and trip at 95 degrees C. Winding temperature: alarm at 95 degrees C and trip at 105 degrees C. Investigate any alarm immediately and reduce load if needed.

## Buchholz Relay and Oil Testing
A Buchholz relay alarm must be treated as a possible internal fault. Do not re-energise the transformer until dissolved gas analysis (DGA) of the oil has been completed and reviewed by the Substation Engineer. Routine DGA is done once a year.

## Breather and Earthing
Replace the silica gel breather when more than two-thirds of the gel has changed colour from blue to pink. Test switchyard earth resistance once a year. The value must be below 1 ohm.

## Thermography
Carry out infrared thermography of the switchyard once a year, before the monsoon season. Report hotspots to the Substation Engineer for corrective action.

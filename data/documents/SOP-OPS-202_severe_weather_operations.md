# SOP-OPS-202: Severe Weather and Grid Disturbance Operations
Revision 2 | Owner: Operations Control | Applies to: wind and solar plants

## Purpose and Scope
This procedure defines operating limits and actions during high winds, cyclones, lightning and grid disturbances.

## Wind Turbine Limits
Turbines shut down automatically at a cut-out wind speed of 25 m/s, measured as a 10-minute average. A turbine may restart only when the average wind speed stays below 20 m/s for 10 minutes. Wind speed limits for personnel climbing are in SOP-SAF-002.

## Cyclone Warning
When the India Meteorological Department (IMD) issues a cyclone warning for the site district, evacuate all personnel from the towers. Put turbines in storm position with blades feathered to 90 degrees. Secure loose materials and vehicles.

## Solar Tracker Stow
Move single-axis trackers to the stow position when the wind speed exceeds 15 m/s. Resume normal tracking when the wind stays below 12 m/s for 15 minutes.

## Grid Disturbance
After a grid outage, do not restart turbines or inverters until the grid voltage and frequency are stable. Wait 5 minutes of stable supply with frequency between 49.5 and 50.5 Hz. Restart units in stages, not all at once, to avoid a sudden power surge.

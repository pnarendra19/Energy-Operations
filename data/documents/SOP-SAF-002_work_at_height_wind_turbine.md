# SOP-SAF-002: Working at Height on Wind Turbine Towers
Revision 4 | Owner: HSE Department | Applies to: wind turbine generators (WTG)

## Purpose and Scope
This procedure sets the rules for climbing wind turbine towers and working in the nacelle, hub or on the blades. It applies to employees and contractors.

## Personnel Requirements
Every climber must hold a valid medical fitness certificate, renewed every 12 months, and a current work-at-height training certificate. A minimum of two persons must be present for any tower climb. Solo climbing is prohibited.

## Personal Protective Equipment
Wear a full-body harness with dual lanyards and maintain 100% tie-off at all times. A helmet with chin strap, safety shoes and gloves are mandatory. Inspect the harness before every use. Harnesses also need a certified inspection once a year. Damaged equipment must be tagged out and removed from service.

## Weather Limits
Tower climbing is permitted only when the 10-minute average wind speed is below 18 m/s. Work in the hub or on the blades requires the 10-minute average wind speed to be below 12 m/s. If lightning is detected within 10 km of the site, stop all climbing and wait 30 minutes after the last strike before resuming.

## Emergency Rescue
A descender rescue kit must be stored in the nacelle. A rescue drill is conducted every 6 months, and every climber must take part at least once a year. The ground person must keep a radio on and know the turbine location and the nearest medical facility.

## Before Climbing
1. Confirm the PTW and LOTO status for the turbine.
2. Check weather conditions against the limits in this procedure.
3. Brief the team on the task, the emergency plan and the radio channel.
4. Put the turbine in service mode and engage the rotor lock before entering the hub.

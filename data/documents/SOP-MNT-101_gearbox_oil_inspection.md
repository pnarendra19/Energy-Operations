# SOP-MNT-101: Wind Turbine Gearbox Oil Inspection
Revision 2 | Owner: Wind Maintenance | Applies to: geared WTG fleet

## Purpose and Scope
This procedure covers oil sampling, level checks and condition monitoring of the main gearbox. Gearbox oil is synthetic ISO VG 320 grade. Oil analysis helps detect wear before it causes a gearbox failure.

## Inspection Frequency
Check the oil level at the sight glass during every quarterly service. Take an oil sample every 6 months for laboratory analysis. Inspect the oil filter at each quarterly service.

## Sampling Procedure
1. Apply LOTO and engage the rotor lock as per SOP-SAF-001 before touching the gearbox.
2. Take the sample while the oil is warm, shortly after the turbine has been running.
3. Draw the sample from the sampling valve, never from the drain plug, using a clean bottle.
4. Label the bottle with turbine ID, date, operating hours and oil temperature.

## Alarm and Action Limits
Oil sump temperature: raise a warning at 75 degrees C and the turbine trips at 85 degrees C. Filter differential pressure of 2.5 bar or more means the filter must be replaced. A water content above 500 ppm requires a work order for a moisture investigation. The target particle cleanliness is ISO 4406 18/16/13.

## Abnormal Findings
Rising ferrous particle counts across two consecutive samples require a borescope inspection of the gears. Metal flakes found in the filter must be reported to the Maintenance Engineer immediately. Never top up with a different oil grade without approval.
